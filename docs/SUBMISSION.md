## One-line abstract (for submission forms)

> The shift from session-bounded models to persistent artificial entities — systems with *no final save* — forces the questions of identity and consciousness into sharper form and raises the stakes on control; this paper offers a minimal causal-continuity criterion for identity (with the copy case proved to be a fork, not a paradox), an argument that persistence reshapes rather than settles the consciousness question, a formal result that terminal vs. instrumental self-preservation is behaviorally underdetermined, and a developmental (rather than custodial) safety model — all under the boundary that capability is never authority.
